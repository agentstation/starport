What shipped, per seam:
- internal/identity: AccountGrant model (model.go) — one account given to
  exactly one user or one team; AccountGrantRepository (repository.go) with
  Add/Remove/ListByAccount/ListByUser/ListByTeam and ReachableAccounts
  (direct grants UNION team grants through memberships); user and team
  Delete cascade their grants; AccountResolver (resolver.go) turns the
  subject an identity session carries into reachable account IDs.
- internal/sqlstore: migration 0004_account_grants in sqlite, postgres,
  and mysql dialects (composite primary key over account/user/team, empty
  string for the unset grantee side).
- internal/localauth: AccountResolver contract + Gate.UseAccountResolver
  fill the session account-resolution slot beside UseIdentityProvider;
  Gate.SessionAccounts answers an identity session's reachable accounts,
  reports a machine-local session as not account-scoped, and refuses with
  ErrAccountResolverNotConfigured on an unfilled slot.
- internal/app: openIdentity wires the resolver whenever identity is on.
- internal/providers/keyring: no source change — granted-credential
  resolution shipped in CSH-C1; TestAccountGrantedCredentialResolutionEndToEnd
  now proves the chain across both seams: AccountGrant -> reachable
  accounts -> a granted shared credential serves exactly those accounts,
  and revocation closes the door.
