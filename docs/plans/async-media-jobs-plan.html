Async media jobs

Status: active · Owner: this plan · Created: 2026-08-26 · Baseline: Starport b5a3d9e, Starmap b1e6ad43 · Proof root: docs/proof/async-media-jobs/ · Next action: AMJ3 adds the Starmap video operation

Outcome

Starport serves a media operation that a provider cannot answer inside one request. A caller submits a video generation request, receives a Starport job identifier, polls that job, and fetches the finished asset from Starport. The job record is the source of truth of that work. Starport prices the job once, at a terminal state, and one tenant cannot queue unbounded provider work.

Progress

Progress appears here when the page loads. The status ledger below is the source of truth.

Architecture

One seam is new. internal/jobs owns the job record, the state machine, and the repository contract. Every other change lands at a seam the model modalities plan already widened.

Scope

Owns, the job seam. The job record and its identifier. The state set and the legal transitions. The repository contract and its storage layout. The retention window of a stored asset. The outstanding job limit in the limits vocabulary.

Owns, the caller surface. The submit route, the poll route, the content route, and the cancel route. Both protocol shapes. The job scope. The console jobs page.

Owns, the provider path. The narrow optional interface that submits and polls one provider job. The Starmap video generation operation and its endpoint facts.

Does not own. The content vocabulary, the media operations, the media units in usage, and the transport registry pattern. The model modalities plan owns each one, and it closed on 2026-08-27.

Non-goals. A general background worker for any Starport task, and a webhook callback to the caller. Neither one has an owner today. The blob storage seam also sits outside this plan. The files API plan owns it, and it closed on 2026-08-27 with its proof at docs/proof/files-api/. AMJ6 stores a generated asset through that seam.

  2. Price a completed job from the Starmap video price.
