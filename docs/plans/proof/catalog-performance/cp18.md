# CP18 — Console cutover

Date: 2026-08-22
Branch: `codex/cp18-spa-cutover`

## What shipped

- The SPA is the only console. `ConsoleConfig` keeps only `Enabled`;
  the `Next` flag and the legacy-only `Title` and `Theme` settings are
  removed with their validation. `openConsole` always builds the SPA
  handler.
- The legacy static console is deleted: `internal/console/handler.go`,
  `handler_test.go`, `static/` (css, fonts, js, vendor), and
  `templates/`. The `PageServer` seam moves into `spa.go`, and
  `spaPagePaths` becomes the single authoritative page-route list.
- CI runs `scripts/verify-console-modernization.sh` and
  `scripts/verify-catalog-performance.sh` in the release-contract gate.
  Both script headers drop their "stays red until cutover" caveat.
- `verify-console-modernization.sh` CM-V19 asserts the palette at its
  shipped location (`components/palette/`, CP12 superseded the proposed
  `components/command/`). CM-V21 (legacy console deleted) passes.
- `verify-openrouter-parity.sh` ORP-V06, ORP-V09, and ORP-V16 retarget
  the same capabilities at the SPA (usage page route, catalog refresh
  call in `console/src`, `Compare.tsx`).
- Docs: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` console routes now list the SPA pages and
  `/assets/*`; the Makefile notice drops the flag reference.

- `GET /` serves the SPA shell (`id="root"`); `/authors` and
  `/models/openai%2Fgpt-4o` return 200 through the SPA fallback.
